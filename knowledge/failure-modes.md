# Failure Modes — how startups die, and how to tell early

Primary sources: `startupmistakes` (the 18), `die`, `pinch`, `aord`, `users`, `startuplessons`,
`persistence`, `startupideas`, `powerful`, `top`, `corpdev`, `lesson`. Each entry has a
**diagnostic question**, the **tell**, and the **fix**. In a checkup, find the one or two
that apply most and rank them. Don't list all of them.

---

## The 18 Mistakes *(startupmistakes)*

| # | Mistake | Diagnostic question | Tell | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Single founder** | Who else is all-in on this with you? | "I'll find a technical cofounder after we raise." | Find a cofounder you've built things with. If you truly must go alone, know it's a handicap. |
| 2 | **Bad location** | Where are you, and who's around you who builds startups? | No peers, no investors, no one who treats startups as normal. | Move to a hub, at least for a while. |
| 3 | **Marginal niche** | Did you pick this space because it's small enough to be safe from competitors? | Hiding from big ideas out of timidity. | Small *start*, big *ambition*. Pick a narrow well that leads somewhere. |
| 4 | **Derivative idea** | Is this "X for Y" because X worked? | Copying a hit, with no organic origin. | Find what you uniquely see. |
| 5 | **Obstinacy** | What have you changed your mind about? | Attached to the first plan; rejects user signal. | Hold the goal and let go of the means. *(persistence)* |
| 6 | **Hiring bad programmers** | Who's building it, and how do you know they're great? | Founders can't judge technical quality, or outsourced v1. | Founders build. Hire slowly. |
| 7 | **Choosing the wrong platform** | Why this stack or distribution platform? | Chosen by fashion, or locked into someone else's walled garden. | Choose what great hackers would choose; avoid dependency traps. |
| 8 | **Slowness in launching** | When could a real user first use it? | "When it's ready." Polishing in private. | Ship the smallest real thing within weeks. |
| 9 | **Launching too early** | Can a user get real value from what you launched? | Nothing works; first impressions burned. | Launch when it does one thing well. |
| 10 | **Having no specific user in mind** | Who exactly is this for? Name them. | "Small businesses." "Gen Z." | Pick a real person and build for them. |
| 11 | **Raising too little** | Does the money get you to a real milestone, or to the next desperate raise? | Runway ends before anything changes. | Raise enough to reach default-alive or a clear next level. |
| 12 | **Spending too much** | What's your burn, and what's it buying? | Office, early hires, ads before product-market fit. | Cut to the bone. Ramen profitable. |
| 13 | **Raising too much** | Is the money forcing you to grow in ways the product can't support? | Big round, big team, unclear product. | Take less; stay small and fast. |
| 14 | **Poor investor management** | Who's actually running the company? | Investors driving strategy; founders handling investors instead of building. | Keep control; be polite and decisive. |
| 15 | **Sacrificing users to (supposed) profit** | Are you squeezing users before you've delighted them? | Paywalls and ads before love. | Make something people want first. *(good)* |
| 16 | **Not wanting to get your hands dirty** | What unglamorous work are you avoiding? | No sales calls, no manual onboarding. | Do things that don't scale. *(ds)* |
| 17 | **Fights between founders** | How do you resolve disagreements? Any unresolved ones right now? | Equity disputes, silent resentment, unclear roles. | Address it now. It's a top killer. *(die)* |
| 18 | **A half-hearted effort** | Is this your only job? | Keeping the day job "until it takes off." | Commit. Half-hearted startups mostly fail. |

## Other killers and warning signs

| Failure mode | Source | Diagnostic | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Default dead and don't know it** | aord | Run `pgcalc.py alive`. Do you make it on current trajectory? | Know the number. If dead, act now. |
| **The fatal pinch** | pinch | ~6 months runway, high burn, flat growth, plan = "raise more"? | Cut burn, get to ramen profitability, don't bet on investors. |
| **Giving up** | die, really | Are you demoralized? | Startups mostly die from founders quitting. Not dying is half the game. |
| **Wrong problem ranked first** | users | Of your three worries, which kills you if ignored? | Rank by lethality. Look for the open door. |
| **Fundraising trouble that's really company trouble** | users | Are investors passing because the company isn't doing well? | Fix growth, and the raise fixes itself. |
| **Sitcom idea** | startupideas | Would anyone use a crappy v1 *right now*? | Find real urgent demand or change the idea. |
| **Tarpit idea** | startupideas | Why did previous attempts fail? | Know the trap or avoid it. |
| **Test-hacking** | lesson, users | Are you optimizing how it *looks* to investors, YC, or press instead of being good? | Stop. Only users' behavior counts. |
| **Business-plan theater** | prepare | Are you writing decks instead of building? | Build prototypes. Impress users, not judges. |
| **Cowering** | powerful | Is the idea less ambitious than it could be, out of fear? | "Just stand up straight." What's the bigger, simpler version? |
| **Mafia market** | powerful | Does the best product win here, or do incumbents control access? | Come in from the side, or pick another market. |
| **Enterprise mud** | powerful | How big must a customer be before they buy? How long do they take to decide? | Sell to smaller, faster-deciding customers first. |
| **Component-supplier box** | powerful | Does someone else own your customer? | Find a way to own the relationship. |
| **Selling brand, not substance** | brandage | If competitors matched you on features, what would be left? | Build a real difference. |
| **Corp dev distraction** | corpdev | Are you in acquisition talks you didn't seek? | Stop unless you want to sell now. |
| **Fundraising eats your mind** | top, fr | What do you think about in the shower? | Raise fast or not at all. |
| **Fake work** | selfindulgence, procrastination | What did you do this week that users would notice? | Measure output by user-visible change. |
| **Big-identity blindness** | identity | Are you defending the idea instead of testing it? | Keep your identity small. |
| **Meanness** | mean | How do you talk about users, competitors, and team? | Mean people fail. Be generous. |
| **Don't listen** | users, word | Do advisors feel like advice bounces off? | Be easy to talk to. Act on counterintuitive advice. |
| **No growth metric** | growth | What's your weekly growth rate on one key number? | Pick it today. |
| **Bad company name/domain** | name | Do you own the .com? | Change the name. |
| **Fake demand** | ds, users | Are users people who love you, or people being polite? | Look for usage and payment, not compliments. |
